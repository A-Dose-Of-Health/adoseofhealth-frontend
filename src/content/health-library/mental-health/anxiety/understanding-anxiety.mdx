## Overview
Anxiety is a normal stress response, but it can become overwhelming or persistent.

## Common symptoms
- Racing thoughts
- Restlessness
- Tight chest or rapid heartbeat
- Trouble sleeping

## Practical self-care
Try breathing exercises, movement, consistent sleep, and reducing stimulants.

## When to seek help
If anxiety interferes with daily life, lasts for weeks, or you have panic attacks, talk to a clinician or counselor.

## FAQs
### Is anxiety the same as panic?
Not always. Panic attacks can be part of anxiety, but they can also occur independently.
