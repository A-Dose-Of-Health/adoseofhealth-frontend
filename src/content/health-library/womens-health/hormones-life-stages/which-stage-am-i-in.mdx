## Which Stage Am I In? — Quick Reference

| Your Situation | Likely Stage | What to Do |
|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| Under 8 with no body changes yet | Pre-pubertal | Normal. Evaluate if over 13 with no breast development. |
| Ages 8–19, periods starting or irregular | Puberty / Adolescence | Irregular cycles common for 1–2 years. Seek help if: periods extremely heavy, pain debilitating, or no period by age 15. |
| Ages 20–39 with regular periods | Reproductive Years | Track your cycle. Seek fertility help after 1 year trying (or 6 months if over 35). |
| Ages 40–50 with changing cycles or new hot flashes | Perimenopause | Normal. Track symptoms. Use contraception — you can still conceive. Seek help if symptoms are severe or bleeding is very heavy. |
| Over 50 with no period for over 1 year | Postmenopause | Focus on bone health, heart health and managing ongoing symptoms. ANY bleeding is not normal — seek urgent evaluation. |

## When to Seek Medical Help at Any Stage

- No period by age 15 (Primary Amenorrhea)
- Periods stop for 3+ months when not pregnant (Secondary Amenorrhea)
- Bleeding is extremely heavy — soaking a pad/tampon every hour
- Bleeding between periods or after sex
- Severe, debilitating pain that OTC medication does not help
- Sudden, severe pelvic pain (could be ectopic pregnancy or ovarian cyst rupture)
- Any bleeding after menopause — URGENT
- Severe mood symptoms that interfere with daily life (depression, anxiety, rage)
- Fever with pelvic pain
