## Overview
Pregnancy is the period when a fetus develops inside the uterus. Prenatal care helps you stay healthy and identify concerns early.

## What prenatal care includes
- Regular clinic visits
- Basic screening tests
- Nutrition and lifestyle guidance

## Self-care and prevention
Eat a balanced diet, rest, stay hydrated, and follow your clinician’s guidance on supplements.

## When to seek help
Seek urgent care if you experience heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, fainting, or reduced fetal movement (later in pregnancy).

## FAQs
### How often should I attend clinic?
Your provider will advise, but visits are typically more frequent as pregnancy progresses.
