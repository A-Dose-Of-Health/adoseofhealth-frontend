## Part 5: Treatment Options — What's Available Now

---

### 1. Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT / MHT)

The most effective treatment for vasomotor symptoms and genitourinary syndrome of menopause. Involves taking estrogen (and progestogen for those with a uterus) to replace what the ovaries no longer make.

| Type | What It Is | For Whom |
|------|-----------|---------| 
| **Estrogen-only therapy** | Estrogen alone (systemic) | Women who have had a hysterectomy — no risk of endometrial cancer without a uterus |
| **Combined therapy (E+P)** | Estrogen + progestogen — the progestogen protects the uterine lining | Women with an intact uterus — essential to prevent endometrial cancer |
| **Low-dose vaginal estrogen** | Creams, tablets, rings applied or inserted vaginally | Women with only vaginal/urinary symptoms — minimal systemic absorption; extremely safe even in most breast cancer survivors |

---

### Routes of Administration

| Route | Forms | Key Advantage |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| **Oral (tablets)** | Pills taken daily | Convenient, widely available — but passes through the liver (higher blood clot risk than transdermal) |
| **Transdermal (through skin)** | Patches, gels, sprays applied to skin | Avoids first-pass liver metabolism — **lower blood clot and stroke risk** than oral HRT; preferred for women with clot risk |
| **Subcutaneous implant/pellet** | Small pellet inserted under skin of abdomen or buttock — lasts 4–8 months (may continue releasing up to 18–24 months) | Consistent hormone levels without daily attention; useful when other methods are ineffective |
| **Vaginal (local only)** | Creams, tablets, rings | Local effect only — minimal systemic absorption; recommended specifically for GSM symptoms |

---

### Benefits vs Risks of HRT

**Benefits**

- Most effective relief for hot flashes and night sweats
- Prevents bone loss and fractures (osteoporosis)
- Improves vaginal health and sexual function
- Improves sleep and mood
- When started within 10 years of menopause, may reduce all-cause mortality
- A Jan 2026 study in *Life Sciences* shows estrogen protects the heart, aorta and kidneys by activating cellular "cleaning" (autophagy) through estrogen receptors

**Risks (Context-Dependent)**

- Small increased risk of **breast cancer** with combined (E+P) therapy — duration-dependent; less with estrogen-only
- Small increased risk of **ovarian cancer** (becomes apparent within 5 years; diminishes after stopping)
- **Oral HRT** increases blood clot/pulmonary embolism risk — *transdermal does not*
- HRT does **not** prevent heart disease or cognitive decline and should not be prescribed solely for these purposes
- Individual risk assessment is always essential

### 2. Non-Hormonal Prescription Medications

| Medication | How It Works | Notes |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| **Elinzanatant (Lynkuet)** — Approved November 2025 | Blocks Neurokinin 1 (NK1) and Neurokinin 3 (NK3) receptors in the brain that regulate body temperature. A completely new non-hormonal mechanism of action. | For women who cannot or prefer not to take hormones. Side effects: fatigue, dizziness, drowsiness. May actually *improve* sleep as a side effect. |
| **SSRIs/SNRIs (antidepressants)** e.g., paroxetine, venlafaxine | Reduce frequency and severity of hot flashes — off-label use | Also beneficial for perimenopausal depression. Not all SSRIs are equally effective for hot flashes. |
| **Gabapentin** | Reduces hot flash frequency — off-label use | Particularly useful for night sweats; can cause dizziness and drowsiness |
| **Ospemifene (SERM)** | A selective estrogen receptor modulator for vaginal dryness and painful sex — taken as a daily tablet | An option for women who cannot use vaginal estrogen |
| **Vaginal moisturisers/lubricants** | Maintain vaginal moisture and reduce friction during sex | First-line treatment for GSM. Non-hormonal — can be used by anyone. Hyaluronic acid gels show comparable efficacy to vaginal estrogen in some studies. |

### 3. Lifestyle Approaches

**Cooling Strategies & Triggers**

- Dress in layers to easily remove when hot
- Use fans or cool water on your face and neck
- Keep your bedroom cool for sleep
- **Common triggers to avoid:** hot drinks, spicy food, alcohol, caffeine, stress, warm environments

**Exercise, Diet & Mind-Body**

- **Regular physical activity** — improves mood, sleep, bone health and cardiovascular health
- **Diet:** Work with a dietitian; moderate caffeine and alcohol; phytoestrogens (soy, flaxseed) — mixed evidence but generally safe
- **Stress management:** Yoga, meditation, deep breathing, CBT
- **Pelvic floor exercises (Kegels):** Help with urinary symptoms and vaginal tone
