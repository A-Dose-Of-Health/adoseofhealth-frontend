## Management & Treatment

---

### Lifestyle (First-line for mild–moderate PMS)

**Nutrition:** Eat complex carbs (boost serotonin); increase **calcium to 1,200 mg/day** — studies show significant reduction in PMS symptoms (sources: milk, yoghurt, leafy greens, fortified plant milks); limit salt (reduces bloating); reduce caffeine and alcohol (worsen anxiety and breast tenderness).

**Exercise:** Regular aerobic activity releases endorphins, improves mood and reduces fatigue.

**Sleep:** Aim 7–9 hours; consistent bedtime helps regulate mood.

**Stress management:** Yoga, meditation, deep breathing, journaling.

---

### Medical Treatments (for PMDD or severe PMS)

**SSRIs (antidepressants)** — the **first-line treatment for PMDD**. Fluoxetine or sertraline can be taken continuously or only during the luteal phase (1–2 weeks before period). Work within *days* for PMDD (faster than for depression).

**Hormonal birth control** — pills suppressing ovulation (e.g., those containing drospirenone and ethinyl estradiol) can eliminate the hormonal cycle that triggers PMDD symptoms.

**GnRH agonists** — In severe cases, temporarily induce menopause to stop the hormonal cycle. Usually short-term.

---

### When to Seek Urgent Help

⚠️ Seek help immediately if:

- You have **thoughts of self-harm or suicide** related to your cycle — this is a medical emergency
- Mood symptoms are affecting your relationships, work or ability to function
- You feel hopeless, overwhelmed or out of control
- Lifestyle changes are not helping after 2–3 cycles

---

### How to Get a Diagnosis — The 4-Step Approach

💡 **Diagnosing PMDD**

1. **Track symptoms daily** for at least two cycles — note mood, physical symptoms and severity.
2. **Look for the pattern** — do symptoms appear 1–2 weeks before your period and disappear after it starts?
3. **Book an appointment** with a gynaecologist, psychiatrist or primary care provider. Bring your symptom tracker.
4. **Be specific:** Say "I think I may have PMDD because my symptoms follow this pattern and they are severely affecting my life."
