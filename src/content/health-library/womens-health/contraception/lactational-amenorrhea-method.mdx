## Lactational Amenorrhea Method (LAM)

Exclusive breastfeeding suppresses ovulation and can prevent pregnancy — **98% effective when ALL THREE conditions are met:**

---

### The Three Required Conditions

**1. You are exclusively breastfeeding** — no supplements, no solid foods, no formula

**2. Your baby is under 6 months old**

**3. Your periods have not returned**

---

### Important Limits

If **any one condition changes**, switch to another contraceptive method immediately.

LAM is free and supports breastfeeding — but only works with strict adherence to all three criteria. The moment your baby begins taking any supplement, formula or solid food, or your periods return, or your baby turns 6 months old, LAM is no longer reliable protection.
