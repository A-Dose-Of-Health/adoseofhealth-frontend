## Fertility Awareness Methods (FAMs)

Track biological signs to identify the fertile window. To avoid pregnancy: abstain or use a barrier method on fertile days. To achieve pregnancy: time sex to coincide with the fertile window.

---

### Signs Tracked

**Cervical mucus** — clear, slippery, egg-white on fertile days

**BBT (Basal Body Temperature)** — temperature rise after ovulation (confirms it occurred)

**Calendar tracking** — for women with very regular cycles

**OPKs (Ovulation Predictor Kits)** — for predicting the LH surge

---

### Effectiveness & Considerations

**Effectiveness:** 99% with perfect use; 76–88% typical use. Requires daily dedication and discipline.

**✅ Pros:** No hormones, no medical procedures, no cost; acceptable to all cultures and religions; increases body awareness; can be used to either avoid or achieve pregnancy.

**❌ Cons:** NOT a "lazy" method — requires daily tracking and strict discipline; difficult with irregular cycles; requires abstinence or backup method on fertile days (~half the cycle); does not protect against STIs; less effective than LARCs or hormonal methods with typical use.

**Best for:** Highly motivated, organised couples with regular cycles. Learn from a trained instructor before relying on it. Use condoms as backup during the learning phase.
