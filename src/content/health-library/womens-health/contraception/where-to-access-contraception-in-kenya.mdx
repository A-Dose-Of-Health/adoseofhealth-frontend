## Where to Access Contraception in Kenya

| Facility Type | Services Offered | Cost |
|--------------|----------------|------|
| **Level 4, 5 & 6 Hospitals** (Sub-county & County hospitals) | Full range: implants, IUDs, injections, pills, condoms, sterilisation | KSh 20–300 (e.g., Kiambu Level 5) |
| **Level 3 Clinics & Dispensaries** | Pills, injectables, condoms; some offer implants and IUDs | Low cost/subsidised |
| **Comprehensive Care Centres (CCCs)** | Integrated HIV and reproductive health services including safer conception counselling | Free for HIV care components |
| **Private hospitals & clinics** | Full range including fertility treatment and ART where available | Varies — higher than public |
| **Pharmacies** | Pills, condoms, emergency contraception; some offer injectables | KSh 20–400 |
| **Online pharmacies (MyDAWA)** | Pills, condoms, EC and ePrEP — delivered discreetly | Market rates; delivery fees may apply |
| **Mobile outreach clinics** | Many counties offer mobile family planning clinics reaching remote and underserved areas | Low/free |
